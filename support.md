# Support

## Raise a request

The [Fieldwise support portal](https://timginvo.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/1)
takes questions, bug reports, feature requests and billing queries. Requests raised
there are tracked and answered in order.

You can also email [timginvo@gmail.com](mailto:timginvo@gmail.com).

## What to include

The Jira site the app is installed on, the name of the app, what you expected to happen
and what happened instead. A screenshot of the gadget's settings answers most questions
on its own.

## Response times

We aim to reply within two business days. Bugs that stop a gadget from rendering are
picked up first.

## Documentation

[Field Stats documentation](./docs.html) covers the settings, date bucketing, pivot
tables and the common problems.
